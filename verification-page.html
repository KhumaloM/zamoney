Next Steps

Your PO has passed pre-verification and is now moving to full verification. Here's what happens next:

  1. ZAMONEY will contact the National Library of South Africa to verify the PO authenticity directly.
  2. A full credit assessment on the buyer will be completed.
  3. Once these checks are complete, the PO will be reviewed for final approval.
  4. You'll receive email notifications at each verification stage.
We may occasionally request additional information to support the verification process. Please ensure your contact details are up to date.
